## Overview

Volunteer project built with Astro, JavaScript, and a custom CMS powered by the GitHub API.

## What is BioConecta

BioConecta is a science outreach museum by Centro Universitário UDF dedicated to communicating biological science topics. Its primary mission is making scientific knowledge and curiosity accessible to diverse communities.
