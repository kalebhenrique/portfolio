## Overview

Developed as a freelance project, this platform was tailored to modernize scheduling, patient care workflows, and electronic health records (EHR) in healthcare clinics. The system empowers both healthcare providers and administrative staff with intuitive, responsive workflows.

## Key Highlights

- **Smart Scheduling**: Interactive calendar with daily, weekly, and monthly views, preventing appointment overlaps and automating patient confirmations.
- **Electronic Health Records (EHR)**: Comprehensive consultation history, prescriptions, medical exam attachments, and secure clinical notes.
- **Financial & Administrative Dashboard**: Procedure reporting and simplified billing control.
- **Responsive & Accessible Interface**: Built with high usability standards, ensuring smooth operation on clinic tablets or reception desktops.

## Impact

The solution reduced appointment confirmation wait times and organized all clinical information flow into a secure, digital, centralized hub.
